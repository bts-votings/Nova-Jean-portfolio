About

More than just a developer.

I like taking an idea from a rough concept, through design, into a functional website that actually ships. That means thinking about the interface, the interactions, and what happens after you hit deploy — not just the first screen someone sees.

Every project I take on is a chance to build something real, responsive and interactive — with clean implementation underneath and a UI that feels considered rather than assembled from templates. I'm continuously learning, and I'd rather ship a working product than talk about one.
